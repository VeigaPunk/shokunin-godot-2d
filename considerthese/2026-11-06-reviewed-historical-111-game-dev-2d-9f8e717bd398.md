# Optical flow assumptions and refinement

Horn and Schunck, [Determining Optical Flow](https://www.sci.utah.edu/~gerig/CS6320-S2012/Materials/Horn-Schunck-1981-orig-article.pdf), 1981, Abstract and §§1–5: local brightness motion needs supplementary constraints; smoothness struggles at occlusions. Utah journal scan, Artificial Intelligence 17, pages 185–203; North-Holland copyright.

Brox, Bruhn, Papenberg and Weickert, [Optical Flow](https://www.mia.uni-saarland.de/Publications/brox-eccv04-of.pdf), ECCV 2004, Abstract and §§1–2: adds gradient constancy and robust piecewise smoothness while retaining nonlinear data constraints. These refine the model’s assumptions; they do not turn estimated image displacement into an account of authored bodily action.
