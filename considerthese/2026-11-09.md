## Three most important points from the window

1. **Painted handles are not the mesh points actually constrained.** `2026-11-06-reviewed-historical-55-…47d0b8092162.md` contrasts Igarashi, Moscovich and Hughes (2005), whose two-stage deformation constrains mesh vertices and only discourages local distortion, with Takeo and Yuki Igarashi's 2009 edge-based formulation, which supports interior handles through barycentric constraints without adding vertices but can still reverse triangles under extreme deformation. The review rule for 2D rigging (relevant to `Polygon2D` / `Skeleton2D`-style workflows, not verified here) is to check intended landmarks separately from constrained vertices, and rigid decorative features separately from whether a handle arrived. The note flags that related papers from one lineage are not independent validation.

2. **Particle systems trade physical fidelity for animator control, and that is a design choice.** `…historical-90…29ce5569059b.md` pairs Reeves (1983), which organizes fuzzy effects as populations with controlled birth, attributes, motion and death, with Sims (1990), whose composable operations let an applied velocity move a particle without changing its stored velocity and whose vortex favors adjustable influence over simulation. The lesson for 2D effects is to separate population bookkeeping from motion treatments and to not assume every look must come from forces. The note is explicit that composed operations are not claimed to be independent or realistic, and that nothing about today's engines was verified.

3. **A seamless boundary does not mean the interior is right, and a faster solver is a different answer.** `…historical-98…a2f1cdcb5098.md` notes that Poisson image editing (Pérez, Gangnet and Blake, 2003) reconciles boundary values with interior variation but does not guarantee the intended interior colour, and that mean-value cloning (Farbman et al., 2009) builds a different membrane without the large linear solve, not an identical harmonic result. This joins the 11-08 theme (plausible image output is not authored intent) and adds a calibration point: an approximation that is cheaper must be judged as its own result, not as "the same thing, faster". The note also records that May 2009 manuscript metadata does not establish the final publisher edition.
