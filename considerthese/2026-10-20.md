## The 3 most important lessons / gains

### 1. The lane turned its own mistakes into an admission bar (round 105, `7d9d974`)

Three knowledge entries were admitted against the new bar and three candidates (clean-checkout import/export/launch, GUT/gdUnit4 exit contracts, a visual canary) were explicitly rejected and routed to r106 for lack of an executed fixture.

### 2. An old engine-behaviour pin was wrong, and the res_uid harness fix routed from r104 landed
Round 92 had pinned `TileMapLayer.get_coords_for_body_rid` as returning `(0,0)` and "unreachable from script". Round 105's `r105_tile_rid.gd` showed the probe had passed the moving body's own RID instead of the collider RID: fed the right argument it returns exact cells (`c5_coords=5,0`, `c21_coords=21,0`), consistent with Godot 4.5+ physics chunking and the documented `physics_quadrant_size = 1` lever (sources S002, S005, S027). The general rule became Q22: a value returned alongside an allowlisted engine `ERROR:` is a refusal receipt, never a state fact. The same round restored the seven `res_uid` rows retired in r104 by adding per-row scratch projects and a writer-to-reader shared-cache control (M11, M28, M29), and admitted a physics-replay boundary entry that deliberately makes no cross-process digest pin, because Godot documents its physics as non-deterministic (S001, S009, S025).
