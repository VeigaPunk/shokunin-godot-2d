## Three most important points from the window

1. **A key position is not its printed label, and key-repeat is not a portable clock.** `2026-11-06-keyboard-position-label-identity.md` (in `55af63f`) separates Godot 3.5's scancode / physical_scancode from Godot 4.4's physical_keycode, Latin keycode and localized key_label. Position-based movement and label-based shortcuts encode different intentions; a displayed prompt must follow the chosen identity. The note also warns that key-repeat configuration varies (including being disabled), so repeated events are not a gameplay clock, and Unicode/IME composition remains a separate path. This is a tagged input-identity contract, not a preference for one binding style.

2. **A teleport needs a new interpolation history, which is not a replay or collision claim.** `2026-11-06-teleport-history.md` pins the Godot 4.7 physics-interpolation tutorial: place the object, then `reset_physics_interpolation` so the stored previous transform equals the current one; for an immediately moving spawn, also set the expected next-tick transform after the reset. That refinement is presentation history only. It does not authorize collision sweeps, deterministic replay, or treating reset as a substitute for correctly timed movement on physics ticks. The note explicitly separates this from the lane's existing replay-boundary note and says headless replay evidence does not validate visible spawn behavior.

3. **Historical method pairs distinguish problem contracts, not "newer replaces older", and access is not a reuse grant.** Across several of the 19 notes: Hart/Nilsson/Raphael vs Dechter/Pearl (`…pathfinding…896ba05d40fa`) separates cheapest-path correctness from search-work / expansion optimality; Baker/Coffman/Rivest vs Jylänki (`…historical-77…`) shows fixed-width open-height packing without rotation is a different contract from finite bins with rotation — finite-bin results cannot silently authorize rotation in a sprite pipeline that cannot reconstruct orientation; Milazzo vs Ford (`…historical-34…`) separates terrain visibility geometry from creature/targeting policy. Every note also records that public links and conditional ACM/classroom notices do not establish unrestricted reuse or AI-training permission. The coverage gain is contract separation plus rights humility, not a new engine measurement.
