Ulichney, [Dithering with Blue Noise](https://cv.ulichney.com/papers/1988-blue-noise.pdf), IEEE 76(1), 1988, 56–79; selected abstract and Introduction, 56–57. Aperiodic patterns without low-frequency grain motivate perturbed error diffusion, unlike rigid ordered patterns.

Ulichney, [The void-and-cluster method for dither array generation](https://cv.ulichney.com/papers/1993-void-cluster.pdf), SPIE 1913, 1993, 332–343; selected §§1–2, 332–334. Spatial construction pursues homogeneous dispersed dots in precomputed threshold arrays. The array remains periodic: improved appearance does not remove tiling.
