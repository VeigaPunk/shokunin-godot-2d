## Three most important lessons from the window

1. **Metadata interpretation and pixel content are separate evidence (PNG color and aspect).** `2026-11-11-png-color-precedence.md` records that PNG color chunks are a priority order, not cumulative corrections (3rd Edition, June 2025: cICP, then iCCP, then sRGB, then cHRM+gAMA; use the highest the decoder understands). `2026-11-12-png-pixel-aspect.md` makes the same point for geometry: pHYs with an unknown unit conveys aspect ratio, not size, and viewers may ignore nonsquare pixels. Together they give the lane a rule for sprite import bugs: identical pixel samples do not prove identical displayed color or shape, so record which chunks survived and what each tool chose before repainting anything.

2. **Godot API semantics are version-pinned graph and lifecycle policies.** `2026-11-12-resource-duplication-graph.md` contrasts the 4.4-stable and 4.5-stable `Resource` contracts: in 4.5, deep duplication recurses into containers, repeated references to one resource stay shared in the copy, and `duplicate_deep` modes (INTERNAL vs ALL) decide whether external-path resources are copied. `2026-11-12-tween-stop-lifetime.md` separates tween controller state from target state: `stop()` resets the tween but does not rewind the property, `kill()` invalidates it, and bound tweens pause off-tree and die when the node is freed. Lesson: predict identity and validity, not just values, and always name the build.

3. **Calibrated values are not measured geometry (SVG pathLength).** `2026-11-12-svg-distance-calibration.md` notes SVG2 (October 2026 CR snapshot) keeps pathLength as a calibration for dashes and distance-along-path but explicitly excludes it from `getTotalLength`/`getPointAtLength`. This extends the earlier stroke-closure reading and continues the cross-note theme of authored intent vs computed result, with a clear warning not to infer Godot importer behavior from browsers.
