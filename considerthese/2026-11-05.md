## Three most important points from the window

1. **Every curated item separates three things: the documentation page, the engine version it describes, and the evidence it gives.** Batch 5 (`2026-11-03-curated-sources-05.md`) does this most clearly for ParallaxBackground to Parallax2D. It pins a 4.2 reference page, the 4.3 beta 1 announcement (May 31, 2024) and 4.7 reference pages, and labels each as a version-scoped page, not an immutable source commit and not proof about the installed binary. It also says an editor conversion does not prove equivalent clipping, zoom response or split-screen behavior. The lesson is that a doc citation is only a claim about one version, and a migration note is only intent. Neither is a measurement.

2. **Each candidate carries a falsifiable question with a negative control, so a later round can fail visibly.** Batch 1 (`2026-11-03-curated-sources.md`) proposes a locale-switching fixture that distinguishes two meanings of one key, checks plural boundaries and preserves placeholders, and keeps glyph and layout checks on a separate real-render path. Batch 5 proposes paired old and new scenes compared at camera rest, negative travel, zoom-out and a scroll limit, with a deliberate direct position write on Parallax2D as the control. All of these are marked "not performed". The gain is that the next round starts from a testable question instead of a topic.

3. **Overlap review is by source family, not just by URL.** Batch 1 notes the official demo repository is a new family in the lane inventory but complements the existing GUT and gdUnit sources, and that the i18n page adds a surface to an existing family. Batch 5 states it adds camera/background ownership to the existing level-import and resource tests and does not duplicate batch 4's LDtk coordinate work. This is a better duplicate check than exact-URL matching, and it keeps the registry growing in distinct directions.
