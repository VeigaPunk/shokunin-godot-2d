## The 3 most important lessons / gains

### 1. Export became a real capability surface (rounds 101–103, +44 CASES)
Three consecutive rounds built out Godot's export path instead of treating it as a black box: the `export_prod` capability lattice (12 rows, `2c658c7`), the `export_launch` matrix (8 rows, `63a3089`), and the `export_div` divergence surface (16 rows across 5 families, `afe5b63`). CASES went 509 → 552 across those rounds. The concrete laws that came out of it (recorded in `LOG.md`):
- Export determinism is warm-to-warm only: the first cold export of an unimported project is corrupt (3672 B, bad ECFG header, not loadable).
- Pack bytes depend on the root path, so shas drift across scratch roots. Pin sizes, magic, census names, and in-process byte compares, never cross-root shas (applied again in r104 as the `s11_pack` de-sha).
- Godot export filters are case-insensitive (`*.GD` == `*.gd`, byte-identical artifacts).

### 2. Calibration discipline: pin the margin, not the constant (round 102)
Round 102 (`63a3089`) issued an append-only attribution correction to round 101: the timeout-probe failure was the *old* 0.75 s bound arm, not the hold-open arm, and it came from host spawn-to-marker latency drifting from about 0.39 s (r22) to about 0.8 s (r101+). The bound was re-derived to 1.25 s with counterfactuals. The same window produced the rule that host-conditional tokens like `joys=N` must never be pinned (three legacy r87/r89 pins folded), and that r101's "540 PASS / 7 FAIL" really meant 5 true failures once by-design reject-arm print lines were discounted. The broader gain is that the lane now corrects its own past verdicts openly rather than silently re-pinning.

### 3. The receipt pipeline got an adversarial gate, plus a res_uid lattice (rounds 103–104)
Round 103 added a receipt-shape META gate (0 flags on 554 audited rows, twice) and measured its boundary: floods and whitespace-PASS are caught at the shape layer, banner/diagnostic forgery stays at the verdict layer, and tab-PASS was invisible. Round 104 (`5f92422`) closed that hole with the P2 census regex `^[ \t]*PASS(?![A-Za-z0-9_-])` (6 of 10 evasion shapes flagged, 0 false positives over 39,806 lines) and an in-battery evasion probe that ran green twice. It also landed a 23-row `res_uid` lattice plus 12 companion adoptions (CASES 552 → 585). Honest caveat: seven `res_uid` rows were retired pre-close because the scratch project's uid cache is shared across rows and lives in the project directory, so XDG isolation alone doesn't fix it; that harness fix is routed to r105.

Also in the window: repo hygiene (`b3be1d1`, `ae23848`, `5191305`) untracked 217 bytecode cache files and widened `.gitignore` after round freeze/scratch trees re-entered history (r79 baseline freeze alone was 615 MB / 43.5k files), and PR #2 added the Devin wiki link to the README.
