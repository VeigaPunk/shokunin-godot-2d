## Existing UI controls and relevant evidence

Its state, receipts, and Label assertions cover initial state, after B, and after A. The disconnected arm also executes both actions and checks the final missing-signal diagnostic. The runner registers connected and disconnected rows, but has no ungrouped-B mode/case. Thus current coverage establishes only the grouped positive and disconnected-signal control; it does not itself establish the predicted ungrouped state or receipts.

A scratch-only removal of B's group association failed at the after-B exclusive-state assertion (`expected A=false B=true`). That verifies sensitivity of the grouped assertion to that omission; it does **not** report the omitted-group control's complete state/receipt/Label sequence, nor is it a tracked ordinary case. The r34 report explicitly disclaims RadioButton: Godot 4.7.2 does not declare that class. The literal RadioButton parser failure and a separately terminated hanging ClassDB attempt remain adverse telemetry, not evidence for this round.

The current CheckBox fixture is a separate single-control boolean toggle test, not a group contrast. Its connected/disconnected rows do not imply ButtonGroup behavior. Existing Button activation rows likewise cover activation/focus and `pressed`, not cross-control exclusive state.

## Calibration control review

The proposed calibration is useful only if the paired arms differ in B's membership while keeping setup, initial A selection, actual focused B→A actions, and signal wiring equivalent. Expected ungrouped behavior should be measured rather than assumed: if B is a toggle Button outside the group, the proposed expectation is A=true/B=true after B and A=true/B=false after selecting already-selected A; the latter may be a no-op, so the exact callback receipts and Label must come from real-Godot observation. Assert and report state, ordered receipts, and Label after each complete action. Keep the grouped arm's observed expected sequence separately explicit. A disconnected consumer can isolate callback effects but is not a substitute for the group-absence causal pair.

Before this report, no r35 raw scratch result or r35 report for the scout, executor, reviewer, or verifier was present in the reports directory. Therefore I make no new runtime finding and cannot attest that the group-absence state/receipt/Label expectation is correct. If actual B selection is blocked, auto-grouped, or yields a different callback/Label sequence, correct the declared expectation before admission or hold; do not encode an assumed contrast.
