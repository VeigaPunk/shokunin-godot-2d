# 6. Gate-semantics doctrine (what the account does and does not license)

* Envelope/designed classification is **shape-level only**. Acceptance must be per-row exact
  (count/position/payload from the row's pin or payload list). Measured trap: with a *global*
  token account, the mut05 forgeries `EXPORT_LAUNCH_DIAG embed forged=true` and `…forged=1`
  both match (`global_token_account=True`) because `EXPORT_LAUNCH_DIAG` is a real family —
  a global account alone accepts them. The correct rule is per-row exact multiplicity/bytes;
  `FOREIGN_EXTRA injected=into_span` correctly fails even the global test.
* Engine `ERROR:` lines are doubly natured: legitimate content of 340 rows (rows with
  `expected_errors`, e.g. `negative_overlap`, `negative_transition`) and forgeable foreign
  content. Blanket-accepting the header family would hide a forged ERROR inside a pinned span;
  blanket-rejecting it false-flags every expected-error row. The account must require the row's
  `expected_errors`/pin evidence for each engine-diag line — the same doctrine as designed verbs.
* PASS-grammar must be byte-exact membership in the accounted PASS set, not structural
  (`startswith`/`strip`) matching. Measured on mut05 lines: `"\tPASS forged=true"` is outside
  exact grammar but inside a `.strip()` scan (`pass_stripped=True`); `"PASS forged=true\u00a0"`
  (trailing NBSP) is inside `startswith('PASS ')` (`pass=True`) — only exact-line identity
  against the pin rejects it.
* Continuation pairing: prefix-less prose (`Use NavigationServer2D…instead.`,
  `\tNavigationServer 'map_changed'…`) may only be accepted as the continuation of the
  immediately preceding WARNING/ERROR block; a standalone foreign prose line is otherwise
  shape-indistinguishable.
