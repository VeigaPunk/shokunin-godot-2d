### 6. Design defects found by measurement (the review payload)

1. **D1 — the "foreign content inside the pinned span" clause is not implementable from stored
   records as they exist.** Records carry `signature` = fragment(s), never a span; V-min's span is
   therefore a single line and the clause flags *nothing* on either mut09 base (measured above).
   Either the record schema gains a `pinned_span` field or the clause is replaced by the
   whole-stdout account (V-rec).
2. **D2 — "zero PASS-lines ⇒ fail-closed" as worded false-positives 64/554 green rows** (16 with no
   `output` key at all). The account must declare the zero-PASS class per case (measured table).
3. **D3 — a boolean multi-PASS allowlist is evadable**: `s3b` appends a forged PASS line to an
   allowlisted row and V-min accepts it. The account must be a byte-exact multiset census.
4. **D4 — accounting PASS lines by "contains a pin fragment" accepts duplicates and edits**: `s4b`
   (legit line + trailing space, which still contains the fragment) escapes V-rec v1. Closed by the
   measured PASS-count table plus byte-exact census.
5. **D5 — banner accounting must be exact-bytes, index-0, count-1.** Measured: one banner variant,
   538/538 at index 0. This is what catches `s1a` (forged banner) and `s1b` (duplicate).
6. **D6 — same-family forged designed-verb lines remain open** (`s2b`): the account is a *first
   token* vocabulary, so a forged payload under a legitimate verb is invisible. Closing it needs a
   per-verb payload grammar (fields per arm); until then this is the gate's documented ceiling.
7. **D7 — the verb vocabulary is large and round-tagged (124 tokens over 379 cases).** It must be a
   frozen literal table regenerated whenever fixtures change; an unregistered case with new verbs
   flags by construction (fail-closed co-update law).
