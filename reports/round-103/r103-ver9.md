## 5. Risks / implications for the orchestrator

1. **8 s bound is the binding constraint** (`run_battery.py:1930`; the 60 s exemption is keyed to the `__godot_export*`
   pseudo-script names and cannot be reached by a `.gd` fixture). Measured iso arm 4.76-5.12 s across loads 1.85→11.4
   (≈3 s headroom, stable — the arm is dominated by CPU-independent I/O/wait). But any unlanded fixture that exports
   twice (s03 multi-preset) or exports plus parses (s04) must be timed on scratch **before** its tuple freezes; a row
   that exceeds 8 s is reported by the harness as `error=TIMEOUT after 8 seconds`, fails `code == expected_exit`, and
   takes the count floor with it.
2. **Pin hygiene**: never include `proj=` (absolute host path) or `xdg_a_store=` (order-dependent) in a pin. The r102
   `export_launch` rows avoid paths entirely; the s02 fixture deliberately leaves `proj=` last and unpinned.
3. **Scratch-root growth (observation, not a gate)**: under the battery neither `EXPORT_DIV_ROOT` nor `EXPORT_LAUNCH_ROOT`
   is set, so the fixture roots at `OS.get_user_data_dir()/export_div_s02` (~73 MB binary + logs per battery run, i.e.
   under `~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/MechanicOneShotTest-<stamp>/`). `/tmp` is never used for the artifact, which
   is what keeps the EDQUOT-hostile mount out of the path.
4. `cases_integrity` prefix addition is **fail-closed**: with the rows present it is True (cell B); dropping the family
   from CASES+results makes it False (cell A). It does not protect the *count* — that is the floor clause's job.
