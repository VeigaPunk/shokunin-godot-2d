### 3.3 Defect measured in the landed tooling: `--min-rows N` silently drops the equality conjunct

`w5_export_div_gate_splice.py:63-68` builds the min-rows form as `count_lines = f'            >= {min_rows}),'`,

the equality (cell C) but cannot see a synchronized shrink (cell N of the earlier run). The amended clause keeps both
conjuncts — that is the shipped `r103-V11-export_div_floor_amend.patch`.
