## PLAN (5 lines, as written before work started)
1. enumerate every probe/verification entrypoint the lane ships and classify by invocation shape (godot headless scene vs python vs shell).
2. time each with wall-clock, N>=3 runs, record min/median.
3. compare against the lane's `<5s requirement`; flag every probe whose median exceeds it.

## 0. What the "<5s requirement" actually is (observation, from source)

| artifact | bound | source |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary CASES row (script `X.gd`) | **8 s** | `run_battery.py:2231` `timeout_seconds = 60 if script.startswith("__godot_export") else 8` |
| `__godot_export*` rows | 60 s | same line |

| timeout-probe arms | 1.25 s / 3.0 s | `:5671 old_timeout_seconds = 1.25`, `:5673 timeout_seconds = 3.0` |

**So "the <5s requirement" is not a single declared constant in the battery.** The two 5 s numbers that
exist are fixture-internal (`RUN_BOUND`) and the export-launch child bound; the row bound is 8 s. This
shard therefore censuses against **5 s as the soft per-probe envelope** the seat was asked about, and
separately reports the hard 8 s bound. [inference: the seat's "<5s requirement" refers to this soft envelope]

### Cost drivers for the over-budget rows [observation → inference]
- `phys2d_backends_lattice` (7432 ms): enumerates physics backends / builds lattices in one boot. Next
  backends arms (`dummy_timeout` 3864, `gate_matrix` 3807, `dummy_degenerate` 3011) are ~2× cheaper → the

- `export_div_s01/03/04/05` and `export_prod` rows (4.8–6.0 s): each fixture performs **one or two nested
  `godot --export-debug` subprocesses** on top of its own boot. Direct nested-export measurement: 2.1–2.4 s
  per export (§5). Two exports + boot + harness ≈ the observed 5–6 s. [inference]
- `export_launch --arm=probe` (6.0–6.1 s): three launches in one row (stock + editor contrast + missing
  sidecar), each ~1.3–1.5 s. [inference]

### Mutation content (not applied — orchestrator's call)
The over-budget rows are all **legitimately over**, not stuck: none is bound-limited. The only lever that
would cut them is splitting the multi-export rows into one-export-per-row (e.g. `export_div_s01_ext` →
`ext_a`/`ext_b`), which costs more rows and more total wall, not less. **Recommendation: no change**; the
8 s hard bound already gives 1.08× headroom on the worst row and every overrun fails closed with a
timeout class. [inference]

### The single genuine anomaly: the hold arms are bound-limited, not work-limited
`descendant_pipe_probe --spawn-hold` run *standalone with no bound* takes **6882 / 6919 ms** — the spawned
descendant inherits the captured stdout/stderr pipes and keeps them open after the direct child exits, so
`communicate()` cannot return. Under the battery's real sequence (`communicate(timeout=3.0)` → `kill()` →
`wait`) the arm costs **exactly 3.002–3.003 s** and `timed_out=True`. Same for `--spawn-forge-hold`
(3.003/3.003) and `--spawn-errforge-hold` (3.002/3.003). [observation]

**This is by design and correctly fail-closed**: the row exists to prove that a pipe-holding descendant
is classified as a timeout (`run_battery.py:2770-2790` comment says so explicitly). The cost is the price
of the claim, not waste. [inference]

Contrast — the *non*-holding arms are cheap and do not touch the bound:
`--spawn-detach` 868/873 ms, `--spawn-sigkill` 832/839 ms, `--spontaneous-crash` 1470/1690 ms (rc=-6),
`godot_error_probe` 889/920 ms, `--spawn-allowforge` ~0.8 s, `--spawn-splitforge` 0.983 s,
`--spawn-seamforge` 0.935 s, `--spawn-midlineforge` 1.040 s, `--spawn-errforge-release` 0.839 s,
`--emit-errforge` 0.907 s, `--spawn-forge-release` 0.961 s.

### The pure-Python meta probe is free
`receipt_shape_audit` over all 554 rows = **2.6 ms** (3 reps: 2.7/2.6/2.6 ms), no Godot boot. The
receipt-shape gate adds nothing measurable to the battery. [observation]

## 6. Verdicts

- **Cost census: complete for 552/554 CASES rows + all 17 meta rows + the 2 export-class rows.**
- **<5 s envelope**: holds for **98.4 %** of CASES rows; 9 rows exceed it (1.6 %), worst 7432 ms.
- **Hard 8 s bound**: **0 violations**; worst row has 1.08× headroom. Fail-closed by construction
  (an overrun yields a timeout class, never a silent green — r103-ver11 §2 agrees).
- **Budget sink is the meta-probe family, not the CASES rows**: ~22 s/run (production) of which 9.0 s is
  three deliberately bound-pinned hold arms; my loaded-host replay puts the same family at ~82 s.
- **Mutation content offered, not applied**: §3 "Recommendation: no change" and §4 "by design". No
  fixture or battery edit is warranted by this census. Scripts left at
  `reports/round-104/mutations/w8_replay.py` and `w8_analyze.py` for the orchestrator.
