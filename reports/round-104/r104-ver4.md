## 9. What is still open / bounds the exploit (labelled)

* **Practical bound on E23/E25 (observation, verified).** The forgery loses the row's `pid=`/`path=`

  **This does not remove the hole**: a forger who preserves `pid=`/`path=` (they are printed by the
  fixture, so they are copyable) keeps both layers green. What is proven is narrower and still fatal
  to G2's claim: *the shape gate and the process classifier do not authenticate the receipt's
  content*, which is exactly what G2 asserted was impossible.
* **E4/E5 residual (observation):** a receipt re-emitted on a whitespace-prefixed line still passes
  even under P1, because `lstrip()` normalises it into an accounted line that contains its own anchor.
  Closing it means *rejecting* whitespace-prefixed PASS lines rather than normalising them, which
  collides with legitimate indented PASS continuations — needs the C2a false-positive census first.
  **Flagged to C2a as an interaction, not solved here.**
* **Inference, not measured:** the attacks are tampered *stored records*. This seat did not make a
  fixture print such output at runtime (no Godot process launched). Whether a fixture can be driven
  to emit `ERROR: <pin>, <junk>` with exactly one `ERROR:` is untested here.
* **Unknown:** whether `sf_discriminations` or any other harness channel independently re-derives a
  row's receipt from the artifact rather than the stored stdout. Only `classify_process` and
  `receipt_shape_flag` were examined.
