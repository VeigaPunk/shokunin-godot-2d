## 2. What the patch inserts — two hunks, both pure insertions

**Hunk 1** `@@ -1855,6 +1855,16 @@` (module level, after `RECEIPT_ANSI_ONLY_RE`):

```python
RECEIPT_PASS_LINE_RE = re.compile(r"^[ \t]*PASS(?![A-Za-z0-9_-])")
RECEIPT_PASS_CANONICAL_RE = re.compile(r"^PASS ")
```

**Hunk 2** `@@ -1901,7 +1911,17 @@` (inside `receipt_shape_flag`, `run_battery.py:1902-1904` at HEAD):
the census line `pass_lines = [line for line in output if line.startswith("PASS ")]` becomes a
`RECEIPT_PASS_LINE_RE.match` census, followed by a new clause that flags any censused line the
canonical form does not match with `why: "pass_line_separator"`.

Three consequences, all by design (the clause fires *before* the accounting clauses, so the widened
census also feeds the grammar and count clauses):

* a `PASS`-shaped line with a TAB / NBSP / EOL separator or leading whitespace is now **visible** to
  the count, anchor and grammar clauses, and separately labelled;
* `PASS` followed by a **word character** (`PASSED`, `PASS_B`) stays invisible — a different token,
  deliberately not censused (matches the negative-lookahead);
* no classifier, no other gate clause, and no CASES row is touched (+18/−0).
