reproduced here.

**Recommendation:** land **exactly one** C2(a) patch, in the **1-site** shape, with the **narrow**

## 4.5 Worked example: the live instance (measured against a landed sibling fixture)

At the time of writing, five C1 fixtures exist untracked in `corpus/fixtures/` — `res_uid.gd` (353
lines), `res_uid_s01.gd` (251), `res_uid_s02.gd` (287), `res_uid_s04.gd` (473), `res_uid_w07.gd`
(303) — and **none** is registered in `CASES` yet (`grep -c res_uid corpus/fixtures/run_battery.py`
→ `0`). The first four each have exactly one `print("PASS` site; `res_uid_w07.gd` has three.

`res_uid_w07.gd`'s helper `_p()` (line 50) prints one PASS line per call, and **7 of its 13 arms
call it twice**: `census`, `builtin`, `custfile`, `cache`, `cachebust`, `api2`, `importremap`. The
other six (`stale`, `nouid`, `collision`, `mkpng`, `persist_a`, `persist_b`) print one. Replayed
against the **landed** gate with realistic single-line pins:

| arm | #PASS | landed verdict | new audit flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| `census` `builtin` `custfile` `cache` `cachebust` `api2` `importremap` | 2 | **FLAG** | `unaccounted_pass_lines` |
| `stale` `nouid` `collision` `mkpng` `persist_a` `persist_b` | 1 | silent | none |

The declaration fix, measured exactly (grammar entries are the `(token1, token2)` pair of each PASS
line, and a declared row is checked by grammar **only** — pin anchoring is skipped for it):

| `RECEIPT_MULTI_PASS_GRAMMAR[case]` | observed pairs | landed verdict |
|---|---|---|
| undeclared | `[('res_uid_w07','census'), ('res_uid_w07','census')]` | `unaccounted_pass_lines` |
| `(("res_uid_w07","census"),)` — one entry | same | **`pass_line_grammar_mismatch`** |
| `(("res_uid_w07","census"), ("res_uid_w07","census"))` — two entries | same | **silent** |

**Observation:** the declaration must carry **one pair per PASS line**, not one pair per row — a
single-entry declaration converts the failure from `unaccounted_pass_lines` to
`pass_line_grammar_mismatch`, i.e. declaring is not enough; it must be counted correctly.
**Inference:** for these seven arms the cheaper fix is to concatenate each arm's two lines into one
PASS line (which also keeps the row's own pin as the anchor), since a grammar row loses pin
anchoring entirely.
