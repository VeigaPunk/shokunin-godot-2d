## What this proves / does not (labels)

- [obs] **Swap of expected include/exclude sets must REJECT** — demonstrated with the harness's
  own substring predicate against captured real bytes, on the own-driver arms (F3) and on both
  landed PASS lines (V1/V2).
- [obs] Inversions that change real export bytes (role/value swaps) are caught by content pins
  (F4; V1/V2).
- [obs] On this build, export filter glob matching is **case-insensitive** (`*.txt` ≡ `*.TXT`,
  `*.md` ≡ `*.MD`); a pure case flip is behavior- and pack-byte-invisible (same-root `cmp`).
  Detection therefore rests on the echoed filter literal in the receipt (echo tier). The
  landed fixture emits the literals, so its full-line pin catches V3.
- [inf] The 8 s battery bound margin (~2 s at load 18-24) is thin; a loaded host could time s04 out.
- [unk] Pattern syntaxes/extensions outside what the two probes measured (both probes covered
  `*.txt`, `*.TXT`, `*.TxT`; E4's ladder additionally covers `CASE/*.txt` and `res://case/*.txt`)
  are unmeasured here; no claim made.
