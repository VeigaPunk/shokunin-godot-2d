## F4 — Capture-loss hypothesis excluded (so the fix is not a capture patch)

Line `run_battery.py:5350` reassigns `raw_stdout` from the post-kill `communicate()`,
looks like it discards the pre-kill partial. It does not:
- CPython 3.14.7 `subprocess.py`: `_fileobj2output` is created only when
  `not self._communication_started` (2115-2125) and persists; the second
  `communicate()` returns the cumulative join (2163-2190) → `more_out` is the full
  stream, nothing lost.
- Empirically: `exc_stdout == more_out` string-identical in **16/16** bounded replica
  runs; where the marker is absent, both are banner-only.
Therefore the r101 absence is real (unwritten at kill), and no change to the capture
line is needed.

Rationale (margins vs measured envelope, load ≤18.4):

Rejected alternatives:

  flake-prone under a load spike; not chosen.
- `1.4`: also passes; needlessly narrows the delay-side margin framing; not chosen.
- Expectation repair (drop `marker_observed` from old_rejects): deletes the
  controlled-delay discriminator (a bare slow startup would also "reject") — no.
- Retry/envelope: 25% steady miss reproduced at 0.75; retry masks the knee and adds
  ~1.5s/run — no.

Naming caveat: `old_timeout_seconds`/`slow_clean_old_bound` nominally refer to the
pre-r26 0.75. Keeping the name with the new value is minimal; a rename to
