## 0. Row contract (read-only recon, run_battery.py @ sha in receipts)

- CASES row = `(name, script, flags, expected_exit, signature)`; `execute()` runs
  `godot --headless --path <scratch> --script res://<script> -- <flags>`
  (run_battery.py:1912) with an 8 s bound, merges stdout+stderr, and the row verdict is
  `classify_process(code, error, output, expected_exit, signature)` (run_battery.py:1775-1792):
  `code == expected_exit and error is None and signature in output and no unrelated ERROR: ...`.
- The committed `probe_export_launch` block (run_battery.py:4510-4601) writes the launched
  app's `export_launch.gd` inline (`extends Node` + `_ready()` + optional print + `quit()`),
  exports `--export-debug Linux/X11`, runs the binary with `--headless`, and its verdict
  requires the positive arm's `same_signature_assertion` (marker printed exactly once) plus
  the negative arm's clean exit without the marker.
- Therefore the harness-dead class ("fixture silently no-ops, prints nothing") has two
  surfaces: (A) a CASES-row executor script blanked to a no-op — the pinned signature is
  absent from 101 bytes of engine banner; (B) the launched app script muted — the positive
  launch assertion goes false while the other verdict clauses stay true (the pack-sha
  clause still differs: root-path dependence, see §2).
