## Findings / observations

* F1 (fixed in fixture): `DirAccess.copy_absolute` does not preserve the executable
  bit (copied binary `-rw-r--r--` -> `Permission denied`, rc=126). Fixture now
  `chmod +x`es the isolated copy (`/usr/bin/chmod` verified present).
* F2: the missing-pck refusal is a *non-self-exiting* process (killed at 6 s and
  10 s bounds; error printed immediately). Bounded with `timeout 2` in the fixture;
  class print observed at ~0.1 s, so the bound cannot lose the evidence.
* F3: embedded pack is 4 B shorter than the same project's standalone pack
  (2036 vs 2040) and its `file_base` header differs — cross-format byte equality
  is false; pin trailer structure and sizes, never cross-format shas (r101 law).
* F4 (inference, not pinned): `pack_bytes`/`bin_bytes` are stable here across 3
  roots, but they are project-content dependent; the row signature stops before them.
