this race, nothing else. (The driver's post-kill second `communicate` returns the accumulated
pipe output; verified: hold arm SIGKILLed at 3.005 s still shows the marker in raw_stdout, and
46 historical old-arm receipts show marker=true at elapsed 0.753.) [obs+inf]

Bound-window diagnostic (`python3 mini_driver.py bound-diag --bound 0.75 1.0 1.5`) [obs]:
delayed-clean arm, timed_out=true and marker_observed=true at ALL three bounds
(0.756/1.005/1.504 s elapsed). The delayed process exits cleanly at ~2.2 s, so any bound in
roughly [0.85, 2.1] restores `old_rejects` while preserving "old bound rejects the delayed
clean startup" semantics — supporting evidence for the D3 fix seat; I changed no fixture.

Historical recalibration of implied margin [inf]: r101 new-arm elapsed (2.346/2.294) minus the
1.5 s delay minus the measured quit tail (0.05–0.11 s) implies those launches' t_marker ≈
0.68–0.79 s. The old arm is a separate launch (separate draw); in both r101 runs its draw lost
the race. Two loses in a row ≈2 draws from a distribution straddling 0.75 (P(miss) ≈ 1/3 in my
samples, higher under load). No leaked-state mechanism is needed. [inf]
