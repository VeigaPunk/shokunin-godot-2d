## 6. Pollution correlation verdict (evidence either way)

- **Controlled own-load test:** P1 (ambient, load 18.2–19.1) vs P2 (same project + 8 own cpu
  spinners, load 20.2–25.8): bound misses 3/8 vs 2/8, Fisher two-sided **p=1.000**; flush-over-bound
  1/8 vs 0/8, p=1.000. Added CPU pollution did **not** increase miss rate or marker latency (P2 flush
  median 0.662 is the fastest phase).
- **Census correlation (small n, time-confounded, reported as-is):** within-phase Pearson
  flush~godot +0.67/+0.42/+0.59; flush~load1 −0.10/+0.29/−0.85 (sign flips). No stable monotone
  relation to godot count, zombie count, or load.
- **Load-band coverage:** misses occur at every observed band (8.6 → 26.2) and D2's clean sequence
  missed at load 6.5–8.2; conversely (sibling D2 data) 8/8 misses in its own window. The miss needs
  no pollution explanation — it is the bound sitting inside the boot/flush distribution.
- **Conclusion:** evidence does **not** support "lingering godot/zombies cause the probe red".
  The pollution present during my runs (18–39 godot processes, 5–26 zombies, load 6→26) neither
  flips the hold arm (6/6 green throughout) nor monotone-shifts the old arm's miss rate.
