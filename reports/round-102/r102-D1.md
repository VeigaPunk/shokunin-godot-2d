while `slow_clean` arms pass". **Observed data say the opposite.** Reading both

Driver logic (`corpus/fixtures/run_battery.py:5375-5387`, read at r102):
`old_rejects` requires `old_positive["marker_observed"]`; `hold_rejects` requires
`hold_open["marker_observed"]` (True) + `timed_out` (True) + `inner_classification
is False` (True, timeout sets `error` non-null; `classify_process` :1791) +
`direct_child_alive_after_cleanup is False` (True). Therefore **hold_open passed
all four of its conditions in both runs; the FAIL is caused solely by
`slow_clean_old_bound.marker_observed=False`** (old_rejects=False → meta FAIL).
The r101 adjudicator's line "hold_open_new_bound times out with marker observed,
both runs — a deterministic host-state class" (r101-adjudicator.md:81-86) reads
the expected pass shape of hold_open as the failure; it mis-attributes.

"Byte-identical arm results" is near-true in shape only: elapsed values differ
(2.294 vs 2.346 etc.; the two marker=False old_bound runs are 0.753/0.753).

## 5. Verdicts per question asked by the brief

- hold_open_new_bound host-state-dependent? **No — observationally stable
  green.** 11 recorded runs (9 pre-r101 sampled + r101×2) + my 5/5 = marker always
  observed, always killed -9 at the bound. Margin over boot now: 3.0-0.814 ≈
  2.19s. It would need ~4x today's boot latency to fail.
- slow_clean arms: new_bound stable green (exit-at ≈2.3s vs 3.0s bound, ≥0.6s
  margin). old_bound is the red arm — intermittent red class tied to
  boot-to-marker (0.68-0.81s) vs bound 0.75s.
- Was r101's two-run red deterministic? **No** — it is a race; my 5 reps split
  2/3, sweep 2/3. r101 just drew two reds (reps are ~50/50 under today's load).
- Expectation rot? No — expectations describe the same intended semantics; the
  implicit host-speed assumption broke. Minimal repairs (for the C2/author
  seat; their call): (a) raise `old_timeout_seconds` to ~1.25 (window
  [0.9, 2.2], preserves both discriminations — rejected-clean-startup +
  marker-captured — with ≥0.4s margin both sides), or (b) envelope: drop the
  `marker_observed` requirement from `old_rejects` (weakens the discrimination:
  a never-booted process then also "passes" the rejection arm — not advised
  given today's data). Also note the driver's timeout branch REPLACES
  `raw_stdout` with post-kill remainder (:5350) — fine today (banner+marker in
  remainder when observed) but any repair should re-check it; do not pin
  elapsed decimals.
