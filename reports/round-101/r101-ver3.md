## s09 — subdirectory census semantics
PCK v4 layout (decoded empirically, md5-verified): `GDPC` + u32 pack_version=4 + godot 4.7.2 + pack_flags=2 + u64 file_base=112 + u64 dir_offset; directory at EOF: u32 count, per entry u32 padded path_len (NUL-padded bytes), u64 offset **relative to file_base**, u64 size, 16-byte md5 of stored bytes, u32 flags; `dir_end == filesize`. Census parser: `/var/tmp/r101-VER3/pck_census.py` (all entries md5ok=True on 8 packs; paths sorted lexicographically in the index; data offsets in discovery order).

| pack | preset | entries | notable |
|---|---|---|---|
| `out/ver3.pck` | all_resources | 9 | `sub/b.gdc` + `sub/b.gd.remap` in place; **no** `.txt`; `project.binary` 0 B (glitch); unbootable |
| `out/cold.pck`/`cold2.pck` | all_resources (fresh cache) | 9 | project.binary 168 B; index identical structure |
| `out/ver3_hidden.pck` | all_resources (+`.hidden_dir/h.gd`) | 9 | **hidden dir excluded** even for a `.gd` resource |
| `out/ver3_txt.pck`/`txt2.pck` | `include_filter="*.txt"` | 12 | adds exactly `root_payload.txt`, `sub/a.txt`, `sub/deeper/c.txt`; basename glob matches any depth, structure preserved; `sub/deeper` appears as a real dir |
Empty dirs contribute no entry (flat file table); `.godot/` engine entries counted separately.
