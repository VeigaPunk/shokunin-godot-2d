The **pinned signature is lines 3–19 as ONE multi-line string** (17 lines). Three clauses, each measured (§4):
* **contiguity** — a forged extra stdout line anywhere between `SEQ_BEGIN` and `SEQ_END` breaks the adjacency the
  substring test needs, so the row rejects. This is the clause that closes F2's `m_seq` shape.
* **digest** — binds the 14 cell lines by content (catches line-count-preserving edits even under a shorter pin).
* **census** — recomputed inside the fixture against `FRESH_PINS`, i.e. the 14 `r100_gui_*` CASES pins **embedded by
  value**, so any harness↔fixture pin drift changes the census line (and the digest).

Classifier semantics used (read-only, cited): `run_battery.py:1698-1701` builds `output = proc.stdout + proc.stderr`
(stdout first), and `:1725-1742` `classify_process` = `code == expected_exit and error is None and signature in output`
plus the `ERROR:` allowlist and the `SCRIPT ERROR:`/`Parse Error:` bans. Two consequences: (a) a multi-line `signature`
is expressible and is a contiguous-substring test; (b) stdout and stderr are captured separately, so an engine `ERROR:`
line cannot interleave into the stdout span (measured: stderr arrives after all stdout, §3).
