## Measured law (new, was claimed-but-void in r95)
Y-translation of the 5-body pile **is order-invariant at 60 tps**: `offy=+1000` and `offy=-1000`
reproduce the y=0 ladder, `distinct_ticks=7`, `last_change_tick=64`, `t2_vec`, `t2_relpos` and
`t64_relpos` **exactly**, while the absolute-y reads move by the offset and the origin query goes
empty (`n_at_0=0`). The x-translation text signature is the y=0 signature by design (relative
frame), so the row pins the *measured* absolute-y and hit-count keys, not just the echo.
